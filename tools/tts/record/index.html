Narration Recorder

In the app: Settings → Copy recording script, then paste here. Most of the script is enumerated from the game's catalogs, so you do not have to play through anything first.

Pick app/assets/audio/narration/ and takes are written straight in. Otherwise they download one by one.

    Load a script to begin.

    Hit record once and read the whole list, leaving about a second of silence between lines. Stop when you reach the end. The take is split on those silences and each piece is named for you.

    #LineLength

    space records and stops · enter saves and moves on. Use this to redo anything the split got wrong.
